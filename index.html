01 — About

Resources move the world. We make sure Western students understand how.

The Western Energy and Resources Club is a student-run organization for anyone curious about how energy and raw materials get financed, produced, traded and regulated — and about the careers that sit behind those decisions.

We are deliberately cross-faculty. A geology student who can read a resource estimate, an Ivey student who can build the NAV model, and an engineering student who understands the process flowsheet each see a different part of the same asset. WERC puts them in the same room.

No prior experience is expected. Most of our members join knowing only that the sector is interesting; they leave with a working vocabulary, a few mentors, and something concrete on their résumé.

  • Open to every faculty and every year, undergraduate and graduate
  • No application or interview to become a general member
  • Executive and analyst roles recruited each spring

Our focus areas

  • 01

    Energy & Power

    Oil and gas, nuclear, renewables, utilities and the power markets that tie them together.

  • 02

    Mining & Metals

    Exploration through production, with a focus on the critical minerals behind electrification.

  • 03

    Commodities & Capital Markets

    Trading, equity research, project finance and how resource assets are actually valued.

  • 04

    Sustainability & Policy

    Decarbonization, ESG reporting, Indigenous partnership and the regulation shaping all of it.
