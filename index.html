01 — About

Resources move the world. We make sure Western students understand how.

The Western Energy and Resources Club is a student-run organization for anyone curious about how energy and raw materials get financed, produced, traded and regulated, and about the careers that sit behind those decisions.

No prior experience is expected. Most of our members join knowing only that the sector is interesting; they leave with a working vocabulary, a few mentors, and something concrete on their resume.

  • Open to every faculty
  • No application or interview to become a general member
